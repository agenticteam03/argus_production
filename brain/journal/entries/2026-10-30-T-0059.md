## 2026-10-30 - T-0059: which inventory targets exist and run; Podman, systemd units and processes

Type: feature (monitoring, API, UI) · ACR-0044 (proposed)
Who: conantkd (+ agent)
Context: the investigation form listed all 17 inventory targets, most of them lab scenario containers that are not
deployed; the operator could not tell which exist. The map showed a stopped container as running (its restart count
still reads). Argus could not watch or investigate an application outside Docker.
Decision / result:
- Every observer says, per declared target, `argus_target_present` and `argus_target_up`; no series is unknown, never
  absent. The exporter for containers (Docker's 404 = absent), the host probe for systemd units (unit file + cgroup) and
  declared processes (`comm` or `pidfile`, never a command line) on its token-protected `/metrics`.
- The map's boxes get `presence`; `GET /api/v1/targets` gets `state` from the same readings; the form hides `absent`
  targets behind "Show N targets not on their host" and marks the stopped ones. The inventory still decides the list.
- Podman through `ARGUS_CONTAINER_SOCKET` (its Docker-compatible socket); same gateway code.
- `service.status`, `process.status` (offered to no model class), class `target_down` (RB-023, `evidence_pack`), alert
  `TargetDown`; the add-capability checker accepts the `service` and `process` kinds.
Consequences: deploy order (both hosts): render the target config, rebuild the target services, copy the host-probe token
to `/opt/argus-v18/secrets/prometheus/host_probe_token` (nobody, 400), reload Prometheus (new job and rule), then the
core and the UI. Until the exporter is rebuilt every state is `unknown` and nothing is hidden. The UI contract samples
`topology.json` and `topology-incident.json` were regenerated whole (their shape changed) and now also carry two
dependency links the inventory gained earlier (client → api, jobworker → cache).
